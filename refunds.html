Refund Policy

Effective date: August 2026

This policy covers the services of Homefield Growth LLC and works together with our Terms of Service. The short version: you pay for results already delivered, you can dispute any lead, billing errors are refunded in full, and you can cancel whenever you like with 30 days' notice.

Onboarding fee (sometimes called a setup fee)

Your one-time onboarding fee covers the infrastructure, data preparation, and campaign buildout required to launch your account. Because this work begins shortly after payment, the onboarding fee is non-refundable once work has started.

Lead billing

We bill weekly, each Friday, for the qualified leads delivered during that week. Every invoice is itemized — each billed lead is listed with its full reply thread, so you can see exactly what you're paying for. Since you're only billed for results already delivered, these charges are non-refundable once the dispute window below has passed.

Per-campaign products — such as just-listed and just-sold campaigns for real estate agents — are billed at the flat rate in your agreement on the Friday after the campaign runs, under the same rules.

Lead disputes

If you believe a lead didn't meet the criteria outlined in your agreement — it doesn't express interest, is a duplicate, or is outside your territory — let us know within 7 days of delivery. Verified invalid leads will be credited or removed from your invoice. We review every dispute in good faith; since the reply thread is attached to every billed lead, most disputes take one look to resolve.

Cancellation

You can cancel at any time with 30 days' written notice — an email to brian@homefieldemail.com is enough. Cancellation takes effect 30 days after we receive your notice. Leads delivered, and per-campaign products run, through the end of that notice period are billed on the following Friday under the rules above; no further charges occur after that. Leads already delivered, including their reply threads, remain yours.

Billing errors

If you believe you've been charged in error, contact us within 30 days of the charge and we'll review it promptly. Verified billing errors are refunded in full to your original payment method.
