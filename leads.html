The leads

Not a form fill.
A sentence they typed themselves.

A Homefield qualified lead is a homeowner in your territory who replies to the outreach expressing interest — asking for a quote, an estimate, a valuation or an offer, asking what you'd charge, or asking to be called. Each lead arrives with name, email, address, estimated home value, timing signal, supplemental phone where available, and the full reply thread.

The definition

A qualified lead is a reply that shows interest.

Specifically: a homeowner in your territory who replies to our outreach expressing interest — asking for a quote, an estimate, a valuation, or an offer, asking what you'd charge, or asking to be called. Every reply is read and graded before it counts. There's no judgment call to argue about at invoice time, because the line is bright: interest bills, everything else doesn't.

  • The homeowner wrote the words — no form, no incentive, no gimmick
  • Graded before delivery, so your bill and your inbox match
  • One price per lead. Nothing else on the invoice.

You are billed for

  • Asks for a quote, estimate, valuation or offer
  • Asks what you'd charge
  • Asks to be called

Never billed

  • Bounces
  • Auto-replies
  • Unsubscribes
  • Wrong contacts
  • Hostile replies
  • "No thanks"

The record

The full record, attached while the reply is still warm.

The reply doesn't arrive alone. Each lead arrives with the property context behind the send, drawn from the same licensed database of 275M+ homeowner records we target from — home value and the timing signal that triggered the send — so whoever picks up opens the conversation already knowing the house and the timing.

  • Delivered within minutes of the reply, not in a nightly batch
  • Pushed to your CRM by webhook — no CSV exports, no copy-paste
  • The full thread comes verbatim, so follow-up starts mid-conversation

Delivery

Where do leads get delivered?

Every qualified lead goes to all three at once, within minutes of the reply.

Your inbox

The reply and the full lead record, in the email account your team already lives in.

Slack ping

A notification in the channel your team watches, so someone can respond while the reply is minutes old.

CRM webhook

A structured record pushed straight into your CRM. It's a contact with a task, not a spreadsheet row.

No portal

There's no Homefield dashboard to log into and no new tool to learn. It works with the tools you already use.

After the reply

Yours forever. That's the whole policy.

A reply generated in your territory belongs to your business — not rented to you for one phone call. Full follow-up rights, no expiry.

  • Every reply in your ZIPs is yours — never resold, never reused, never shared
  • Months-later conversations included — a homeowner who circles back in October on a March reply is still your lead
  • No per-contact meter — follow up, quote, and remarket on your own schedule

The quality commitment

The incentive math does most of the enforcing: you're billed per qualified reply, so weak targeting costs Homefield before it costs you.
