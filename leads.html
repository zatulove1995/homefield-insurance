The leads

Not a form fill.
A sentence they typed themselves.

A Homefield lead is a homeowner who read a personal email about their upcoming renewal and wrote back. Here's exactly what counts, what one looks like, and where it lands.

The definition

A qualified lead is a reply that shows interest.

Specifically: a homeowner in your territory who replies to our outreach expressing interest — asking for a quote, asking what you'd charge, or asking to be called. Every reply is read and graded before it counts. There's no judgment call to argue about at invoice time, because the line is bright: interest bills, everything else doesn't.

  • The homeowner wrote the words — no form, no incentive, no gimmick
  • Graded before delivery, so your bill and your inbox match
  • One price per lead. Nothing else on the invoice.

You are billed for

  • Asks for a quote
  • Asks what you'd charge
  • Asks to be called

Never billed

  • Bounces
  • Auto-replies
  • Unsubscribes
  • Wrong contacts
  • Hostile replies
  • "No thanks"

Delivery

Where it lands

Every qualified lead goes to all three at once, within minutes of the reply.

Your inbox

The reply and the full nine-field record, in the email account your team already lives in.

Slack ping

A notification in the channel your producers watch, so someone picks up the phone while the reply is minutes old.

CRM webhook

A structured record pushed straight into your CRM. It's a contact with a task, not a spreadsheet row.

No portal

There's no Homefield dashboard to log into and no new tool to learn. It works with the tools you already use.

After the reply

Yours forever. That's the whole policy.

A reply generated in your territory belongs to your agency — not rented to you for one phone call. Full follow-up rights, no expiry.

  • Every reply in your ZIPs is yours — never resold, never reused, never shared
  • Months-later conversations included — a homeowner who circles back in October on a March reply is still your lead
  • No per-contact meter — quote, follow up, and remarket on your own schedule

The quality guarantee

If lead quality drops, tell us and we re-cut the targeting — tighter home-value band, different renewal window, different ZIP mix — until the replies are worth your producers' time again.

The incentive math does most of the enforcing: you're billed per qualified reply, so weak targeting costs Homefield before it costs you.

No make-goods to negotiate. The pricing model is the guarantee.
