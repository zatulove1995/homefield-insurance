1. The service

Homefield runs outbound email campaigns to homeowners within a territory you select, and delivers qualified replies to you. “Territory” means the set of ZIP codes assigned to you at onboarding.

2. Qualified leads and billing

You are billed per qualified lead: a reply from a homeowner in your territory expressing interest in a quote. We do not bill for bounces, auto-replies, unsubscribe requests, out-of-territory contacts, duplicates, or replies declining interest.

Rates are set per industry and quoted to you in writing before onboarding; the rate steps down at 51 qualified leads in a calendar month and again at 300. Invoices are issued monthly in arrears, itemized with each qualified lead and its reply thread, and are due on receipt.

3. Disputes about lead quality

If you believe a lead was billed in error, tell us within 10 days of delivery. If we agree, we credit it against your next invoice — and if a billing error results in an overcharge, we refund it.

4. Territory exclusivity

While your account is active and in good standing, we will not run campaigns for another client in your assigned ZIP codes. Replies generated in your territory belong to you, including follow-up conversations after delivery. Territory is released 30 days after your account becomes inactive or unpaid.

  • You will handle homeowner data lawfully and honor any opt-out a consumer gives you.
  • You will not resell, redistribute, or share leads outside your agency.
