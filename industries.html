Industries

Four audiences.
One homeowner each.

Homefield serves four kinds of businesses: home services companies (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, solar, remodeling), home insurance agencies, real estate agents and real estate investors. Each reaches the same homeowner at a different moment — roof or system age, the modeled policy-renewal window, just-listed and just-sold, or ownership tenure and equity — with one business per industry per ZIP.

Home services

The roof hits year 18. That's the week we write.

Roofing, HVAC, plumbing, solar, remodeling — every home's systems age into replacement range on a schedule you can model. A roof past year 18, a furnace or condenser past year 12: we work it out from the build year, permit history and property records, then send when the numbers say the conversation is due. Seasonal peaks and storm waves layer on top of the calendar.

  • Aging-system targeting — outreach goes to the homes whose roof, HVAC or major systems are in replacement range, not to everyone on the street
  • Estimate requests in their words — the homeowner writes back asking what a replacement runs, and the thread arrives with the record
  • One business per industry per ZIP — your ZIPs are locked against other roofers; a roofer, an agency, an agent and an investor can share a ZIP, two roofers never can

Home insurance

Thirty to forty-five days before the policy renews.

Nobody outside the carrier knows the renewal date — and we won't pretend otherwise. What we do know is when the home was purchased, because closing dates are public record, and home policies typically renew on the closing anniversary. We model the renewal month from that and time the email to land inside the 30–45 day window, when the homeowner is about to see the new premium and hasn't re-shopped in years. After hail or wind in your ZIPs, an additional wave offers homeowners a coverage review — within state rules on post-storm outreach.

  • Timed to the window — most sends land 30–45 days before the modeled renewal, and the ones that drift are still a relevant note to a homeowner who pays for insurance
  • They ask for the quote — the reply is a homeowner asking what you'd charge, not a form fill sold to whoever's dialing fastest
  • Your ZIPs locked from other agencies — one agency per ZIP, held while you're active, and every reply in your territory is yours

Real estate agents

Just listed. Just sold. The whole street hears it.

The moment you list or sell, we email the nearest neighbors — a personal two-step note that references the actual address, not blanket farm copy — and their replies land straight in your inbox. Synced to your MLS, so campaigns fire themselves the moment the listing or closing hits.

  • MLS-triggered — list or close, and the campaign launches on its own within hours; nothing for you to do
  • Two-step sequences under 100 words — every email names the real listing, sale, and street, so replying feels natural
  • Replies in your own inbox — neighbors asking what their home would go for, in their own words, sorted and ready to work

Real estate investors

Sellers who would rather skip the listing.

Long tenure, high equity, absentee owners, landlords who've had enough — these are the signals behind a homeowner likely to sell direct, off-market, as-is. We reach them by email before a sign goes in the yard, and what comes back is an offer conversation with someone who raised their hand, not a resold absentee-owner list every other investor in the county is mailing this month.

  • Off-market sellers before they list — owner-occupancy, tenure and equity filters find the tired landlord and the long-held house before an agent does
  • Offer conversations, not resold lists — the homeowner replies asking what you'd pay and how fast you'd close
  • One investor per ZIP — an agent and an investor can both hold the same ZIP, because they're different industries; two investors never can

FAQ

Straight answers

Can I claim more than one industry?

Yes. Each industry is its own territory lock — an agent who also invests holds a separate lock per industry in the same ZIPs. Pricing and the per-lead rate work the same way for each.

Do the leads say what they actually want?

They reply in their own words, and most name it outright — a quote, an estimate, a valuation, or an offer. The full reply thread is attached to every lead, verbatim, so you're never guessing at what the homeowner meant.

What if my business isn't listed here?

If you serve homeowners at their house, there's almost always a moment in the data we can time it to. Ask on the call — we'll tell you honestly whether the signal exists for your trade before you commit to anything.
