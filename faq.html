FAQ

Straight answers. All of them.

Every question businesses ask before they start, grouped so you can skip to the part you're weighing. If yours isn't here, it's a fifteen-minute call away.

Getting started

Which businesses do you serve?

Four kinds. Home services businesses — roofing, HVAC, plumbing, solar, remodeling and similar trades. Home insurance agencies. Real estate agents. Real estate investors. All four buy the same thing from us: attention from homeowners at the moment they're actually ready. Territory is one business per industry per ZIP, so a roofer and an agent can hold the same ZIP; two roofers cannot.

Is there a contract or minimum?

No. After a one-time onboarding you pay per qualified lead, month to month. Pause or stop whenever — your territory releases 30 days after you go inactive.

How long until my first lead?

About 21 days. Roughly two weeks to set up your campaign and build your list, then replies start arriving in the first sending week.

What do you need from my business to start?

Four things: your branding — business name, how you sign, who the sender is — the ZIP codes you want, a sign-off on the sender identity we'll use, and a 30-minute onboarding call to set the sequence. That's the whole list. Domains, warming, list building, and sending are on us.

Can I see the emails before they send?

Yes. You approve the full sequence at onboarding, word for word, and any copy change after that comes to you before it ships. Nothing sends under your name that you haven't read.

Leads & quality

What exactly counts as a qualified lead?

A homeowner in your territory who replies to our outreach expressing interest — asking for a quote, an estimate, a valuation or an offer, asking what you'd charge, asking to be called. We never bill an auto-reply, a bounce, an unsubscribe, a wrong contact, or a "no thanks."

What if the lead quality drops?

Tell us and we re-cut the targeting. You're billed per qualified reply, so weak targeting costs us before it costs you — the incentives point the same direction.

What close rate should I expect?

Businesses report 20–25% with fast follow-up. Speed is the variable that matters most: get back within a day and those numbers hold; let a reply sit for a week and it decays like any warm lead does. At the close rates businesses have reported, a qualified lead can turn into a customer for roughly four to five times its per-lead rate — though your results depend on your territory and follow-up.

Do leads say what they want?

Usually. Homeowners reply in their own words, and most name it — a quote on the house, an estimate on the roof, what the place would list for, whether you'd buy it as-is. The full reply thread is attached to every lead, so you read exactly what they asked before you pick up the phone. And nothing limits what you sell them: if the reply asks about the kitchen as well as the roof, or the whole house instead of one system, that's yours.

What happens to leads I don't close?

They're yours forever — full follow-up rights, no expiry, no re-sale. Most businesses re-work unclosed replies at the next natural moment — the next renewal, the next season, the next time the market moves: a warm list you already paid for once.

Territory

How exclusive is my territory really?

Completely, within your industry. Once a ZIP is assigned to your business, no other Homefield client in your industry can be sold into it. A roofer, an insurance agency, an agent and an investor can all hold the same ZIP — they're never competing for the same job — but two roofers cannot, and neither can two agencies. Every reply generated in your ZIPs belongs to you, including follow-up conversations months later.

How many ZIPs can I claim?

100 to 500, depending on tier: up to 150 on Starter, 350 on Growth, 500 on Scale. You pick them at onboarding and they lock on signature — held as long as you're active.

What if my ZIPs are taken?

We check availability live on the call, so you'll know in the first five minutes. If a ZIP you want is claimed in your industry, you go on the waitlist for it and we notify you the day it releases. Territories release 30 days after a business goes inactive, so the list does move.

Can I expand later?

Anytime, into adjacent ZIPs that are still open. Expansion is first come, first served like everything else — if you're planning to grow into a specific area, claim it early rather than watching someone else lock it.

Compliance & sending

Is this kind of email outreach legal?

Yes, under CAN-SPAM, which governs commercial email in the US. Every message identifies the real sender, carries a physical mailing address, and honors one-click unsubscribes immediately, with opt-outs suppressed permanently. You review and approve every sequence before it sends — campaigns run at your direction, so ultimate responsibility for your marketing's compliance stays with your business, as our terms spell out; we implement the best-practice mechanics on every send

Do the emails come from my business or from you?

Yours. We send from dedicated sending domains you approve at onboarding, clearly signed by your business — so the homeowner is talking to you from the first sentence. That's why the replies read like conversations, not like lead forms.

Will this hurt my business's email domain?

No — it's never touched. No — your own domain is never used. Outreach runs on separate, dedicated sending domains we register for your campaign, with the sender identity signed off by you at onboarding, so your everyday business email is unaffected. Your own email keeps working exactly as it does today.

How do homeowners' addresses get sourced?

Public-record ownership data — the same records a title company or a direct-mail house works from — paired with contact information licensed from established data providers who certify lawful collection and permissible use, then scrubbed against suppression lists before anything sends. We filter from 275M+ homeowner records by ZIP, home value, home and system age, ownership length, equity band, renewal window for insurance, and owner-occupancy for investors.

Billing

How am I billed?

Weekly — every Friday, in arrears, for the qualified leads delivered that week. The invoice is itemized — every line is a lead, and every lead has its full reply thread attached, so you can audit what you're paying for in one read. (Real estate agents' just-listed / just-sold campaigns work differently: those are a flat rate per campaign, invoiced after the campaign completes — no subscription.)

Can I dispute a lead?

Yes. If a billed lead shouldn't have qualified — wrong territory, no real interest, a misread reply — flag it and we review the thread. If we got it wrong, it comes off the invoice. Most disputes resolve inside a day, because the evidence is a written reply.

Can I set a monthly cap?

Yes. Tell us the most leads you can actually work in a month and we pace the sending to it. You'll never get a surprise invoice because the outreach performed better than expected.

What does onboarding cost?

It's one-time, quoted on the call — the number depends on territory size and setup.
