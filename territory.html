Territory

One agency per ZIP.
That's the whole rule.

Claim 100–500 ZIP codes, and every qualified reply inside them is yours alone — held while you're active, released only if you leave. No auctions, no tiers of exclusivity.

Why it matters

Exclusivity isn't a feature. It's the product.

Every shared lead started life as a good lead — a homeowner who actually wanted a quote. Then the vendor sold that one moment of interest to four, six, eight agencies at once, and by the third voicemail the homeowner stopped being interested in anyone.

The failure isn't in the lead. It's in the crowd around it. So we removed the crowd: when a homeowner in your ZIPs replies, you are the only agency that will ever hear about it — not the fastest dialer, not the highest bidder. The only one. Everything else on this page is just the paperwork around that sentence.

The rules

Three rules. No asterisks.

  1. 01

    Claimed on signature

    You choose 100–500 ZIP codes at onboarding. The moment you sign, they're yours — first come, first served, and a bigger budget doesn't jump the line.

  2. 02

    Held while active

    As long as you're active, no other Homefield client can ever be sold into your ZIPs. The dedupe runs network-wide, across every campaign we send, automatically.

  3. 03

    Released 30 days after inactive

    Go inactive and your territory releases 30 days later. Fair-use, in both directions: territories never sit dead, and nobody can squat on ZIPs they aren't working.

Expansion

Growing? Take the ZIP next door.

Territories aren't frozen at onboarding. Any adjacent ZIP that's still open can be added to yours whenever you want it.

  • Adjacent open ZIPs, anytime — they lock to you the moment you claim them
  • Your tier sets the ceiling — up to 150 ZIPs on Starter, 350 on Growth, 500 on Scale
  • No expansion fee — new ZIPs fold into the same sequence at the same per-lead price
  • The fence protects you too — if the ZIP you want is claimed, it stays claimed while that agency is active

Territory FAQ

The fine print, out loud

Can another agency outbid me for my ZIPs?

No. Territories are first come, first served, and held for as long as you're active. There's no auction, no premium tier, and no phone call a bigger agency can make. Money doesn't move the fence.

Can I reserve ZIPs before I've onboarded?

We'll put a soft hold on your ZIPs through your onboarding call, so nobody claims them out from under you while you decide. After the call, a signature is what locks them — soft holds don't stretch indefinitely.

What happens to my leads if I leave?

They're yours forever. Every reply generated in your territory belongs to you with full follow-up rights — quoting a lead six months after you've left is fine. Leaving releases your ZIPs after 30 days; it never claws back a lead.

We have two offices in one metro. How does that work?

One territory, routed by ZIP. On the Scale plan, multi-office routing sends each lead to whichever office owns that ZIP — one agreement, one dedupe, no internal collisions.
