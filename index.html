Territory

One business per industry per ZIP. That's the whole rule.

The reason shared leads convert badly isn't the lead — it's the four other businesses calling it. So we don't sell the same ground twice in the same industry. A roofer, an agency, an agent and an investor can all share a ZIP; two roofers never can.

  • 100–500 ZIP codes per business, chosen by you at onboarding
  • Locked on signature — held as long as you're active
  • Never sold twice in your industry — deduped across the entire network
  • Expand anytime into adjacent ZIPs that are still open
  • Full follow-up rights — every reply in your territory is yours, forever

FAQ

Straight answers

Is there a contract or a minimum?

No. After a one-time onboarding you pay per qualified lead, month to month. Pause or stop whenever — your territory releases 30 days after you go inactive.

What exactly counts as a qualified lead?

A homeowner in your territory who replies to our outreach expressing interest — asking for a quote, estimate, valuation or offer, asking what you'd charge, asking to be called. We never bill an auto-reply, a bounce, an unsubscribe, a wrong contact, or a "no thanks."

How long until my first lead?

About 21 days. Roughly two weeks to warm sending infrastructure and build your list, then replies start arriving in the first sending week.
