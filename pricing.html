Every tier includes

The tiers change the rate. Not the machine.

The data

275M+ homeowner records, cut by ZIP, home value, home and system age, ownership length, equity band, and household profile — plus the per-industry filters: renewal window for insurance, owner-occupancy for investors. Every tier draws from the same file.

The sending

Dedicated sending domains set up for your campaign and approved by you — never your real domain. A personal, plain-text three-email sequence over 14 days.

The classification

Every reply read and graded. Bounces, auto-replies, opt-outs, and "no thanks" are disqualified — and a disqualified reply never reaches your bill.

The compliance

CAN-SPAM throughout: real sender identity, physical mailing address, one-click unsubscribe honored instantly, suppression maintained, state rules respected.

Pricing FAQ

The billing questions, answered

When am I actually billed?

Monthly, in arrears. Each invoice covers the prior month's qualified leads, itemized — every line is a reply you can open and read.

What if I think a lead was graded wrong?

Flag it. We review the full thread, and obvious misgrades are credited — no argument. The definition is narrow on purpose: if a reasonable person wouldn't read the reply as interest in a quote, an estimate, a valuation, or an offer, you shouldn't pay for it.

Can I pause?

Yes, anytime. Your territory holds for 30 days while you're paused; after that it releases and goes back to first come, first served for the next business in your industry.

Do prices drop as volume grows?

Yes — that's what the tiers are. Your industry's rate steps down at 51 leads a month and again at 300. The rate itself is set per industry, because a roofing lead and an insurance lead aren't worth the same thing.

Is there a cap on what I can spend?

If you want one. Set a monthly cap anytime and we pace the sending to it — you'll never get a surprise invoice because the outreach performed better than expected.
