Does Homefield sell auto insurance leads? No. Here's what we do instead.

  • What Homefield does — emails homeowners in your ZIPs 30–45 days before their modeled home-policy renewal, in your agency's name, with a sequence you approve. Homeowners who reply asking for a quote are delivered to you as qualified leads. One agency per ZIP.
  • What the auto angle is — your approved sequence can invite a home + auto bundle quote. When the homeowner writes back asking what you'd charge, the bundle is yours to work in follow-up.
  • What Homefield does not do — we do not generate auto insurance leads from drivers, we hold no vehicle, registration or auto-policy data, we do not time anything to an auto renewal, and we never label a lead as "shopping auto" unless the homeowner says so in the thread.

Homefield doesn't have auto data — nobody sells a reliable auto renewal signal, and we won't pretend to. What we have is the home policy: a public-record closing date, a modeled renewal month, and a homeowner who is about to re-shop everything. If your agency writes home and auto, that reply is a bundle conversation.

The moment

The home renewal is when a household re-shops everything

Nobody outside the carrier knows the renewal date. We model the renewal month from the public-record closing date — home policies typically renew on the closing anniversary — and time the first email to land 30–45 days before it. Most sends land in the window; some drift, and those homeowners still get a relevant note from a local agency. You're billed only when someone writes back with interest.

Home is the anchor line. J.D. Power's 2024 U.S. Home Insurance Study found a record 6.8% home-insurance shopping rate and that 21% of home customers "definitely will" switch home insurers if they switch auto (Insurance Business, 2024-09-18); in 2022 the same study found about a third of bundlers would (Insurance Journal, 2022-09-21). Its Q2 2025 shopping study put the auto shopping rate at 13.0% and found bundlers shopping and switching at higher rates than non-bundlers (Insurance Business, 2025-07-23). For many households the two policies are one decision, made at renewal — and the home renewal is the one with a date we can model.

EverQuote's own guide for agents makes the same argument from the other side: cross-selling from a home policy to auto is generally easier than the reverse (learn.everquote.com/home-and-auto-insurance-leads, accessed 2026-09-14). We agree — and we start there.

The sequence

Your sequence, your bundle offer

Three short, plain-text emails go out in your agency's name from dedicated, reputation-managed sending domains you approve at onboarding. You approve the sender identity, the subject lines and every line of copy before anything sends — so if you write both lines, your sequence can invite a home + auto bundle quote in your own words. The homeowner replies to your agency. The full thread arrives with the lead, and what they wrote is what you work.

  • You approve every sequence — nothing sends under your name that you haven't read
  • The bundle line is optional — an agency that writes home only simply leaves it out
  • A reply exits the sequence instantly — nobody who answers gets another automated touch

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about home and auto insurance leads

Does Homefield sell auto (car) insurance leads?

Not as a separate product. Every Homefield lead is a homeowner who replied about their home policy. If your approved sequence invites a home + auto bundle quote, the reply is a bundle opportunity — but the timing signal is the home policy, not a car. We don't have auto renewal data, and we'd rather tell you that here than on your first invoice.

Do you know when the homeowner's auto policy renews?

No. We hold no auto-policy, vehicle or registration data and time nothing to an auto renewal. What we model is the home policy's renewal month, from the public-record closing date. For many households the home renewal is when both policies get re-shopped; the bundle conversation happens in your follow-up.

Can my sequence mention the home + auto bundle?

Yes. You approve the sender identity and every email before anything sends, so your sequence can invite a home + auto bundle quote in your own words — or leave auto out entirely. Nothing sends under your name that you haven't read.

How much do auto insurance leads cost?

Third-party guides publish ranges by lead type — shared web lead, exclusive web lead, live transfer — and they are tabulated with their sources, auto rows included, on what insurance leads cost; the marketplaces themselves publish no per-lead price. Homefield doesn't sell auto leads. Its homeowner leads are billed per qualified reply at a rate set by industry and quoted in writing on the call, with bounces, auto-replies, unsubscribes, out-of-territory, duplicates, wrong contacts, hostile replies and declines never billed.

What does a bundle-ready lead record contain?

Name, email confirmed by the reply, property address, estimated home value, the modeled home-policy renewal window, a supplemental phone number where available, and the full reply thread. Nothing about vehicles or an auto policy unless the homeowner wrote it. Phone numbers are supplemental; we make no representation about Do Not Call status, and you scrub before you dial. See our Terms.

Is this for mono-line auto agencies?

No. Without a home policy to anchor to there is no modeled moment and no bundle to invite — an auto-only agency would be paying for homeowner replies it can't write. If you write both lines, or home first, it fits.

How do agents work the bundle in follow-up?

Answer the reply the same day, quote the home policy they asked about, and — if your sequence invited it — ask for the auto declarations page in the same message. The homeowner already told you what they want; the thread is attached.
